Frequently Asked Questions

The HEvOD is a comprehensive repository of evacuation orders, issued by official state and local agencies in response to the hurricanes that impacted the United States between 2014 and 2026. Corresponding to each evacuation order, the database includes information on the type of the order (mandatory or voluntary), the announcement date and time of the order, the effective date and time of the order, and the areas that were the target of the order for evacuation.

Our data is systematically compiled from diverse sources, including official websites and social media accounts of local and state governments and government agencies, as well as news platforms.

It is updated as new data becomes available or when revisions or corrections to the existing data set are identified.

The HEvOD allows for analysis in various areas including evacuation decision-making, policy assessment, effectiveness of evacuation orders and zoning, and communication strategies. Furthermore, by studying multiple hurricanes over time and across different regions, researchers can draw broader, more generalizable conclusions. Ultimately, HEvOD serves as a significant tool for strategic evacuation planning and disaster mitigation during hurricanes.
